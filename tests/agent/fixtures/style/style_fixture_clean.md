# Beacon field notes

Beacon is a shift-scheduling assistant for small cooperatives. It reads the roster you already keep and flags conflicts before the week starts.

## Working with Liberation Labs

We would rather say less than promise more. Beacon is not a compliance checkbox, and it has three views: the week, the person, and the gap. The features are listed in the manual (PDF, 2 pages). Tiers 1–3 use the same export.

Numbers come from your rota. A vendor benchmark is a different thing and we do not use one. The Nottingham office adopted it in May and their offers of help are welcome.

Straight "quotes" and the apostrophe in it's are fine. Setup takes an afternoon: usually less.
